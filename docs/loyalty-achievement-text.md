# Loyalty achievement text, for review

Every Loyalty achievement in EyeWire II, with the line a player sees on the unlock card and in the Trophy Case. Days are total days of science (a day counts when the player makes an edit or completes a cell); they do not have to be in a row.

Rows 2 to 30 are final wording from Ames. Row 40 is her line. Rows 50 to 365 are first drafts.

The source of truth is `src/widgets/badge_definitions.ts` (`LOYALTY_BADGES`). The art and its prompts are at github.com/amyleesterling/badges/tree/main/loyalty; those text files hold the art prompt and the title only, not this player text.
