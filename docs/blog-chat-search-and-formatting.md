Chat is where a lot of Pyr happens: questions about a tricky branch, a link to a cell, a cheer when someone finishes a big one. This week it picked up three additions, all suggested by Krzysztof Kruk. None of them takes up any more of your screen.

## Search the conversation

Click the magnifier at the top of the chat. The line where you type a message becomes a search line, and the chat shows only the messages that match.

- Type one or more words. A message has to contain all of them.
- Put a minus sign in front of a word to leave out messages that have it, like `merge -queue`.
- Put words in quotes to find them together, like `"cut off"`.
- Type `from:` and a name to see one player's messages, like `from:Nseraf`.

Click any result to jump to that message in the conversation, with everything around it. Press **Esc**, or click the magnifier again, to go back to chat.

Search looks through the messages chat has loaded. To reach further back, press **Search earlier messages too** at the top of the list.

## Bold and underline

There is no toolbar to learn. Put a star on each side of the words you want in bold, and an underscore on each side of the words you want underlined:

```
this is *bold*
this is _underlined_
```

Or select some text in the message box and press **Ctrl+B** for bold or **Ctrl+U** for underline. Press it again to take the mark off. You can use both on the same words.

The marks only count around a whole word or phrase. A name with an underscore in it, or a sum written with stars, stays exactly as you typed it.

## Find your own messages

A thin bar now appears beside everything you wrote, which makes your own messages easy to spot when you scroll back. If you would rather not see it, open **Settings** and switch off **Mark my own messages in chat**.
