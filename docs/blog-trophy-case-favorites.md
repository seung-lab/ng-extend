Your Trophy Case fills up fast. A few hundred edits and a few dozen cells in, there is a lot in there you are proud of, and until this week you could only put a handful of it on show. Players asked for more room, so here it is.

## Three kinds of favorite

You can now feature up to sixteen Achievements on your profile:

- **One gold favorite.** The big one at the top of your profile.
- **Five silver favorites.** A row right under it.
- **Ten bronze favorites.** A new, smaller row under those.

To pick one, open your Trophy Case and click any Achievement you have earned. Under it you will find three buttons: **Make gold**, **Add silver** and **Add bronze**. An Achievement holds one star at a time, so giving it a new one moves it to that row.

## Put them in your order

Drag a silver favorite to move it among the silver ones, and the same for bronze. They stay in the order you leave them.

## Arrange the whole case

The Trophy Case has a section for each kind of Achievement: Cell Completions, Editor Achievements, Loyalty Achievements and Special Awards. Move your mouse over a section's heading and a small grip appears. Drag it to move that section up or down, or use the arrows beside it.

The order is saved with your profile, so anyone who visits sees your case the way you arranged it.

## A fix along the way

A few players found they could only add one or two favorites, with room apparently left. An Achievement that was no longer shown was still holding a place. Those places are free again.

Thank you to everyone who asked for this, and to Nseraf for the detailed report that led to the fix.
